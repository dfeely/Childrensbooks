The Danger Game

Franky Brown is pulled inside a dangerous videogame his mum invented, and the Danger Gang has five levels to get through to rescue him.

The Danger Game is a adventure title by Tom Fletcher, illustrated by Shane Devries. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 19. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is The Danger Game about?

Franky Brown finds a mysterious videogame in his garage, starts playing and gets pulled into it. His mum, an inventor, created the game, but it has turned dangerous. Franky’s friends in the Danger Gang, a group of eight kids with secret superpowers, must use their abilities to rescue him by getting through five levels, collecting five danger diamonds and defeating a final boss.

Powers include Mollie’s invisibility, Eric’s shark mode and Katy’s ability to shrink. The book is illustrated by Shane Devries.

Why children enjoy The Danger Game

  • A videogame world is a natural hook for gamers.
  • Superpowers and a level-by-level structure keep the plot clear and exciting.
  • Lots of illustrations help break up the 384 pages.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Ask: “What superpower would you choose, and what would be its weakness?”
  • Design your own game level with obstacles and a boss.
  • Keep a tally of levels as you read.
