Julian Gough

The Irish writer of the Rabbit and Bear picture books.

Julian Gough is a writer. The Irish writer of the Rabbit and Bear picture books. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books are in the current top 20 and where to start.

About Julian Gough

Julian Gough is an Irish writer. He has written five Rabbit & Bear books, illustrated by Jim Field and published in more than thirty languages.

Rabbit’s Bad Habits was shortlisted for the Irish Book of the Year Award in 2016, and its French translation won the Prix Livrentête in 2018. The Pest in the Nest was shortlisted for the Irish Book of the Year Award and the Sainsbury’s Children’s Book Award.

Outside children’s books, he won the BBC National Short Story Award in 2007, wrote the “End Poem” at the close of the video game Minecraft, and has published four novels for adults.

Julian Gough’s books in the top 20
