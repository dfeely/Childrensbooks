## What is Rabbit and Bear: The Bog of Frogs about?

Rabbit is wondering about the night sky: what are stars made of, and where do they go in the day? Bear suggests that Castor can answer with his telescope, but this leaves Rabbit feeling small in a very large world. Rabbit hopes that new friends in the Bog of Frogs can help him see things from a different angle.

It is the seventh Rabbit and Bear book and is illustrated by Jim Field. The publisher lists it for readers from around five and notes it is a good fit for those moving on from picture books.

## Why children enjoy Rabbit and Bear: The Bog of Frogs

- Big questions about space and feelings, told in a gentle, funny way.
- Illustrations on many pages help newer readers.
- A good step from picture books to short chapter books.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “What do you wonder about when you look at the stars?”
- Go outside on a clear evening and look for the Moon and stars together.
- Talk about what Rabbit’s worries feel like and how friends can help.
