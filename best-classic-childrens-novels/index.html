Best classic children’s novels

Heidi, Little Women, Anne and more: classics that are still loved by young readers.

Classic novels are often best shared. Read a chapter at a time, or choose an abridged edition for younger children. These six classics suit readers from about 7 up, and each has stood the test of time.

Tips for reading classics

  • Start with a chapter. Long books feel easier in small pieces.
  • Choose a good edition. Look for clear print and illustrations.
  • Talk about old words. Looking up a few words together can be fun.
  • Try the audiobook. Listen in the car and read the book at bedtime.

What are the best classic children’s novels?

Heidi, Little Women, Anne of Green Gables, The Wind in the Willows and Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland are among the most loved.

What age is Heidi for?

About 8 and up as a solo read, or younger as a read-aloud.

What age is Little Women for?

About 9 and up.

Are older novels hard to read?

Some have old-fashioned language, so abridged editions or reading aloud can help.
