**The Wind in the Willows** is a classic animal fantasy by [Kenneth Grahame](/kenneth-grahame/), first published in 1908. It is number 16 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). Four friends share adventures along a riverbank and in the Wild Wood.

## What is The Wind in the Willows about?

Mole leaves his spring cleaning behind and meets Ratty on the river. Together with grumpy, wise Badger and boastful Toad, they have adventures by the water, in the Wild Wood and at Toad Hall.

It was published in 1908. The prose is old-fashioned, so many families prefer to read it aloud or choose an abridged edition for younger children.

## Why children enjoy The Wind in the Willows

- A cosy, vivid picture of friendship and home.
- Funny scenes with Toad that children love.
- Beautiful descriptions of the seasons and the river.

## Reading and talking tips

- Read a chapter at a time and talk about the old words.
- Ask: which friend would you want to spend a day with?
- Have a picnic by a river or pond.

## About Kenneth Grahame

Kenneth Grahame was born on 8 March 1859 in Edinburgh and died on 6 July 1932. He worked for many years at the Bank of England, where he became Secretary.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
### What age is The Wind in the Willows for?
It suits ages 6 to 10 as a read-aloud, and older children can enjoy it on their own.

### Is it hard to read?
The language is old-fashioned, so abridged editions are a good first step.
