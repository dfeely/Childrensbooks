Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone

Four new demigods enter a restricted part of the camp, and campers start disappearing.

Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone is a mythological fantasy adventure title by Rick Riordan. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 13. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone about?

The book is set during the autumn off-season at the Long Island demigod camp, when few campers are around. Four inexperienced demigods enter a restricted area called the Wild Zone, which wakes ancient dangers and exposes buried secrets. As campers start vanishing, the new heroes must fight monsters and curses and make hard choices while working out where they belong.

It is book one of a new Camp Half-Blood series from the world of Percy Jackson, so fans of Rick Riordan’s earlier stories will recognise the setting.

Why children enjoy Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone

  • A new generation of characters gives returning fans something fresh.
  • Greek mythology is woven into the action, which can spark an interest in the old stories.
  • The mystery of the missing campers adds suspense.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Ask: “Which Greek god or goddess would you like as a parent?”
  • Look up one monster from the book in a mythology collection.
  • Compare it with Irish legends in our guide for another take on heroes and monsters.

Frequently asked questions about Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone

What age is Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone for?

We suggest roughly 9 to 13. It has monsters and some peril, so it suits readers who enjoy adventure stories.

Do I need to have read Percy Jackson?

Not necessarily. It is the first book in a new series, though knowing the camp helps.

Where can we learn about the myths behind it?

Try Greek myth collections from your library. For homegrown stories, see our guide to Irish myths and legends for children.

Where to find it: your local library, an independent bookshop or an online retailer. Library membership is free in Ireland; see our guide to using your library.
