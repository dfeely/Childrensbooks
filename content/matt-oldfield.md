**Matt Oldfield** is a children’s author. A Bristol author who writes football books for children. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books are in the current top 20 and where to start.

## About Matt Oldfield

Matt Oldfield is a children’s author based in Bristol who writes football books for readers of about 7 to 12 and runs literacy workshops for football fans and reluctant readers.

He writes the **Ultimate Football Heroes** and **Classic Football Heroes** biography series with his brother. His non-fiction book Unbelievable Football won the Children’s Sports Book of the Year award in 2020, and he has also written football fiction such as Accidental Football Genius and Undercover Football Genius.

He works with organisations including the National Literacy Trust, the National Football Museum, Beanstalk and Football Beyond Borders.
