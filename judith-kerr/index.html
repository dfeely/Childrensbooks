Judith Kerr

The author and illustrator of The Tiger Who Came to Tea.

Judith Kerr is an author and illustrator. The author and illustrator of The Tiger Who Came to Tea. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

About Judith Kerr

Judith Kerr was born on 14 June 1923 in Berlin and died on 22 May 2019 in London. As a child she fled Nazi Germany with her family and later lived in Britain.

She wrote and illustrated The Tiger Who Came to Tea (1968) and the Mog books, and wrote the novel When Hitler Stole Pink Rabbit (1971), based on her childhood.
