**Lewis Carroll** is an author and mathematician. The pen name of the mathematician who wrote Alice in Wonderland. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Lewis Carroll

Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, born on 27 January 1832 in Daresbury, Cheshire, who died on 14 January 1898. He was a mathematician who lectured at Christ Church, Oxford.

He first told the Alice story on a boat trip in 1862 and published **Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland** in 1865. It was followed by Through the Looking-Glass in 1871.

## Lewis Carroll’s books in our all-time classics list

- [Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland](/alices-adventures-in-wonderland/) (1865): number 9 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A girl falls down a rabbit hole into a world of nonsense and puzzles.

## Other well-known books and work

- Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland (1865)
- Through the Looking-Glass (1871)
- The Hunting of the Snark (1876)
