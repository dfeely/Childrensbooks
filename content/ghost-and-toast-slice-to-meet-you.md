## What is Ghost and Toast: Slice to Meet You! about?

Ghost arrives at a haunted mansion and meets Toast, a talking slice of bread. Ghost cannot remember his life before he became a ghost, so the pair team up to solve the mystery, though they keep getting sidetracked. Their adventures take in evil pastries, a ghost train, giant kittens and gingerbread houses.

The scheming villain is Croissant, who wants to cause chaos. Ghost is the sensible, somewhat exasperated one, and Toast is the cheerful, eccentric one.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “If you were a ghost, what would you want to remember first?”
- Invent a villain from your breakfast table.
- Take turns doing the voices of Ghost and Toast.

### What age is Ghost and Toast for?
The publisher gives 7 to 12.

### Is Ghost and Toast scary?
It has a haunted-house setting but the tone is silly, so it should suit most children who enjoy comic adventures.

Yes. It is aimed at fans of those comics, with fast jokes, big pictures and a lot of daft ideas.
