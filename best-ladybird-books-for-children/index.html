Fairy tales and rhymes (ages 2 to 5)

The Ladybird Tales retell traditional stories in short, clear text. Pair them with nursery rhyme books for first story times. See our guide to nursery rhyme and fairy tale books.

Early reading (ages 4 to 7)

The Key Words books and flashcards are designed to help children learn common words step by step. Use them alongside picture books and shared reading. See books for early readers.

Tips

  • Read the story together first, then let your child read the repeated words.
  • Keep sessions short and fun.
  • Check the level and age before you order.

What age are Ladybird books for?

About 2 to 7, depending on the title. Tales and rhymes suit younger children, Key Words books suit early readers.

What is Key Words with Peter and Jane?

A reading scheme from Ladybird that introduces common words in a series of graded books.

Are Ladybird Tales good for bedtime?

Yes. They are short and suit reading aloud.
