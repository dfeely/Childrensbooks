Frequently asked questions

Which authors are in the current top 20?

This page covers all 17 authors behind the top 20 children’s books we checked on 7 November 2026, from Jeff Kinney and Julia Donaldson to Irish writers such as Cecelia Ahern and Leona Forde.

Are the author pictures photographs?

No. Every portrait is our own illustration of a generic writer at work. They are not likenesses of the real authors.

Where do the biographies come from?

From publisher biographies, reference works, interviews and authors’ own websites. Each author page links its sources, and we only include facts we could check.
