**The Hobbit** is a fantasy novel by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/jrr-tolkien/), first published in 1937. It is number 13 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A small, comfort-loving hobbit is swept into a quest to win back a treasure from a dragon.

## What is The Hobbit about?

Bilbo Baggins is a hobbit who likes quiet comforts. When the wizard Gandalf and a company of dwarves turn up at his door, he finds himself on an adventure to a far-off mountain and the dragon who guards its treasure.

It was published in 1937. Tolkien began the story as a tale for his own children, and the book led to The Lord of the Rings.

## Why children enjoy The Hobbit

- A hero who is brave in surprising ways.
- A big, detailed fantasy world with a map.
- A natural step up for children who loved Narnia or Harry Potter.

## Reading and talking tips

- Draw a map of the journey as you read.
- Ask: when was Bilbo most brave?
- Read the riddles aloud and try to answer them.

## About J.R.R. Tolkien

J.R.R. Tolkien was born John Ronald Reuel Tolkien on 3 January 1892 in Bloemfontein, South Africa, and died on 2 September 1973. He was a professor at Oxford.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
### What age is The Hobbit for?
It suits about 9 and up as a solo read, or younger as a read-aloud.

### Do I need to read The Hobbit before The Lord of the Rings?
It is best to start with The Hobbit, which is shorter and written for younger readers.

### Where can I borrow The Hobbit?
