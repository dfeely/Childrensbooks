Eric Carle

The illustrator famous for his tissue-paper collage picture books.

Eric Carle is an author and illustrator. The illustrator famous for his tissue-paper collage picture books. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

About Eric Carle

Eric Carle was born on 25 June 1929 in Syracuse, New York, and died on 23 May 2021. He made his bright pictures from hand-painted tissue paper that he cut and layered into collages.

His best-known book, The Very Hungry Caterpillar (1969), has been read by generations of children. He helped to found the Eric Carle Museum of Picture Book Art in Amherst, Massachusetts.

Eric Carle’s books in our all-time classics list

  • The Very Hungry Caterpillar (1969): number 2 in our best children’s books of all time. A tiny caterpillar eats its way through the week and turns into a butterfly.
