**Dr. Seuss** is an author and illustrator. The pen name of Theodor Seuss Geisel, creator of The Cat in the Hat. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Dr. Seuss

Dr. Seuss was the pen name of Theodor Seuss Geisel, born on 2 March 1904 in Springfield, Massachusetts, who died on 24 September 1991. He wrote and illustrated dozens of children’s books.

**The Cat in the Hat** was published in 1957 and **Green Eggs and Ham** in 1960. Other books include How the Grinch Stole Christmas! (1957) and Oh, the Places You’ll Go! (1990).

## Dr. Seuss’s books in our all-time classics list

- [The Cat in the Hat](/the-cat-in-the-hat/) (1957): number 10 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A very rainy day turns upside down when a mischievous visitor arrives.
- [Green Eggs and Ham](/green-eggs-and-ham/) (1960): number 14 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A grumpy character is asked again and again to try a plate of green eggs and ham.

Start with The Cat in the Hat or Green Eggs and Ham.
