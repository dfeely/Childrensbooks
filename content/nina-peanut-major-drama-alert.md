**Nina Peanut: Major Drama Alert** is a illustrated school fiction title by Sarah Bowie. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 15. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

## What is Nina Peanut: Major Drama Alert about?

Nina is annoyed when her mum asks her to show a new boy, Ethan, around town. She remembers him as a childhood rival, but he is now older and, to her surprise, rather appealing. The school’s end-of-term production is Romeo and Juliet, and Nina wants the lead opposite him. Her rival, Megan, wants the same part, so the two compete to be the top drama student.

It is the fourth book in the series and follows Is Amazing, Mega Mystery Solver and Epic World Tour Era. Sarah Bowie illustrates her own stories.

## Why children enjoy Nina Peanut: Major Drama Alert

- A dramatic heroine, a school play and a friendly rivalry make for a lively read.
- Illustrations keep the pace quick.
- A story about wanting a role and handling disappointment will ring true for many tweens.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “Have you ever wanted something that a friend also wanted?”
- Read a short scene from Romeo and Juliet together, or watch a child-friendly version.
- Make your own comic panel of a dramatic moment from your week.
