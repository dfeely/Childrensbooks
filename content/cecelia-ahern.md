**Cecelia Ahern** is a novelist and children’s author. The Irish novelist behind P.S. I Love You, now writing for children. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Cecelia Ahern

Cecelia Ahern is an Irish novelist who made her debut at 21 with **P.S. I Love You** (2004), which became a bestseller and was made into a film starring Hilary Swank. Her second novel, Love, Rosie, was also filmed. A news report on her children’s launch gives 21 novels in 30 languages, with 25 million copies sold.

**Detective Thingy Majiggy** is her first children’s series, for readers aged 7 and over. It follows ten-year-old Mia, who moves into a foster home with Detective Lucy and can communicate with everyday objects, which help her solve mysteries. HarperCollins is the publisher.

## Cecelia Ahern’s books in the top 20
