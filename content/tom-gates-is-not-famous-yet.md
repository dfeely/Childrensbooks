**Tom Gates Is Not Famous (Yet)** is a illustrated diary fiction title by Liz Pichon. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 12. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

## What is Tom Gates Is Not Famous (Yet) about?

Oakfield School is excited about a rumoured visit from a local theatre group that may bring a real celebrity. Tom writes a comedy with parts for his friends, but Marcus, who loves attention, becomes more difficult than usual.

As ever, it is told in diary form, packed with doodles, lists and jokes, and it asks whether friendship matters more than fame.

## Why children enjoy Tom Gates Is Not Famous (Yet)

- Doodles and lists make pages quick to read.
- Tom’s school world, band daydreams and family are comforting for regular readers.
- A theme of friendship vs fame gives older readers something to think about.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “Would you rather be famous or have great friends, and why?”
- Keep your own doodle diary like Tom’s.
- Write a short play with parts for your friends.

## About Liz Pichon

Liz Pichon writes and illustrates the Tom Gates series, which began in 2011 with The Brilliant World of Tom Gates.

## Frequently asked questions about Tom Gates Is Not Famous (Yet)

:::faq
### What age is Tom Gates Is Not Famous (Yet) for?
We suggest roughly 7 to 12. The diary format suits early chapter-book readers as well as older children.

### Do I need to read the other Tom Gates books first?
No. Each book has its own story, and the characters are easy to get to know.

### How long is the book?
The hardback runs to 256 pages, but the drawings and short entries make it a quick read.
