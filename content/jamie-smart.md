**Jamie Smart** is a comic creator, author and illustrator. The comic creator behind Bunny vs Monkey. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Jamie Smart

Jamie Smart is a children’s author and illustrator whose comics have appeared for more than 15 years in The Dandy, The Beano, The DFC and The Phoenix. He draws cute characters and places them in ridiculous, colourful worlds.

**Bunny vs Monkey** began as a long-running weekly strip in The Phoenix and has been collected into bestselling books. His other work includes the Superhero Bunny League books (2015), Find Chaffy, and Looshkin: The Maddest Cat in the World (2022).

Earlier in his career he was the full-time writer and artist on Desperate Dan for five years, and he created and edits the free annual comic Moose Kid Comics.
