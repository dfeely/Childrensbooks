**C.S. Lewis** is an author and academic. The Belfast-born author of the Chronicles of Narnia. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About C.S. Lewis

C.S. Lewis, born Clive Staples Lewis on 29 November 1898 in Belfast, died on 22 November 1963. He was a scholar who taught at Oxford and Cambridge.

He wrote the seven Chronicles of Narnia between 1950 and 1956, beginning with **The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe**. His books for adults include Mere Christianity and The Screwtape Letters.

## C.S. Lewis’s books in our all-time classics list

- [The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe](/the-lion-the-witch-and-the-wardrobe/) (1950): number 5 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). Four children step through a wardrobe and find a snowy land called Narnia.

## Other well-known books and work

- The Chronicles of Narnia (seven books, 1950 to 1956)
- Mere Christianity
- The Screwtape Letters

## Awards and recognition

- The Last Battle won the Carnegie Medal in 1956
