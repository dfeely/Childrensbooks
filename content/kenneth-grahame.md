**Kenneth Grahame** is an author. The author of The Wind in the Willows. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Kenneth Grahame

Kenneth Grahame was born on 8 March 1859 in Edinburgh and died on 6 July 1932. He worked for many years at the Bank of England, where he became Secretary.

He published **The Wind in the Willows** in 1908. His earlier books include The Golden Age (1895) and Dream Days (1898).
