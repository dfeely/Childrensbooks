Percy Jackson books in order

The five Percy Jackson and the Olympians books in order, and where to go next.

Percy Jackson and the Olympians is a series of five fantasy novels by Rick Riordan, starting with The Lightning Thief, published in 2005. They follow a boy who learns that the Greek gods are real and that he is the son of one of them. They suit readers aged about 9 to 13.

What to read after the five books

Riordan’s later series share the same world: The Heroes of Olympus, The Trials of Apollo, and others. A newer story in the Percy Jackson world, Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone, is in our current Top 20 children’s books.

Which age is Percy Jackson for?

About 9 to 13. The stories are fast-moving and funny, with action and some scary moments. They make a good read-aloud for younger children if you are happy to skip the scariest bits.

If you like Percy Jackson

Try Artemis Fowl, The Hobbit and Harry Potter.

What order should I read Percy Jackson in?

Start with The Lightning Thief and read the five Olympians books in order. Then move on to The Heroes of Olympus.

How many Percy Jackson books are there?

Five in the original Percy Jackson and the Olympians series, with several follow-up series in the same world.

What age is Percy Jackson for?

About 9 to 13.

Who wrote Percy Jackson?

Rick Riordan, an American author who taught middle school before writing full-time.
