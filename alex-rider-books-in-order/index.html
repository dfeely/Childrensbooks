Alex Rider books in order

Every Alex Rider spy novel in reading order, from Stormbreaker to Ark Angel and beyond.

Alex Rider is a spy thriller series by the British author Anthony Horowitz. The first book, Stormbreaker, was published in 2000 and follows a teenage boy recruited by British intelligence. Ark Angel is the sixth book. The series suits readers aged about 10 and up, as it contains peril and some violence.

Is Alex Rider suitable for my child?

The stories are thrilling and fast, with action and some darker moments. Most children enjoy them from about 10 or 11. Check the first book yourself if your child is younger.

What order should I read Alex Rider in?

Publication order, starting with Stormbreaker. Read Russian Roulette last.

Where does Ark Angel come in the series?

It is the sixth Alex Rider book, published in 2005, between Scorpia and Snakehead.
