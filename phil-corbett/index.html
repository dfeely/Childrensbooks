Phil Corbett

The comics creator behind Ghost and Toast and Kitty Quest.

Phil Corbett is a comics creator, writer and illustrator. The comics creator behind Ghost and Toast and Kitty Quest. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

About Phil Corbett

Phil Corbett is a comics creator who was born in England and now lives in France. He writes and illustrates comics and is the creator of the Kitty Quest graphic novels.

He was the final writer and illustrator of Korky the Cat, a long-running character in the UK comic The Dandy, and has worked on projects for Cartoon Network, Disney, Nickelodeon and Nintendo.

His Ghost and Toast books include Ghost to Coast and Slice to Meet You!
