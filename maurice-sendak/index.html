Maurice Sendak

The author and illustrator of Where the Wild Things Are.

Maurice Sendak is an author and illustrator. The author and illustrator of Where the Wild Things Are. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

About Maurice Sendak

Maurice Sendak was born on 10 June 1928 in Brooklyn, New York, and died on 8 May 2012. He wrote and illustrated Where the Wild Things Are (1963), which won the Caldecott Medal in 1964.

His other books include In the Night Kitchen and Outside Over There, and he illustrated the Little Bear books by Else Holmelund Minarik. He won the Hans Christian Andersen Award in 1970 and the Astrid Lindgren Memorial Award in 2003.

Maurice Sendak’s books in our all-time classics list

  • Where the Wild Things Are (1963): number 3 in our best children’s books of all time. A boy sent to bed without supper sails away to a wild island and learns he can go home again.
