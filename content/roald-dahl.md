**Roald Dahl** is an author. The Welsh-born storyteller behind Matilda and Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Roald Dahl

Roald Dahl was born on 13 September 1916 in Llandaff, Cardiff, Wales, to Norwegian parents, and died on 23 November 1990. He served as a fighter pilot in the Royal Air Force in the Second World War before becoming a writer.

His children’s books include **Charlie and the Chocolate Factory** (1964) and **Matilda** (1988). Most were illustrated by Quentin Blake.

## Roald Dahl’s books in our all-time classics list

- [Matilda](/matilda/) (1988): number 6 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A bright girl with unkind parents finds a surprising power.
- [Charlie and the Chocolate Factory](/charlie-and-the-chocolate-factory/) (1964): number 15 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). Five children win golden tickets to see inside Willy Wonka’s famous chocolate factory.

## Other well-known books and work

- James and the Giant Peach (1961)
- Fantastic Mr Fox (1970)
- The BFG (1982)
- The Witches (1983)

## Where to start

For younger children, start with Fantastic Mr Fox, then try Matilda.
