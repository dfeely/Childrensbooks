**The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe** is a fantasy novel by [C.S. Lewis](/cs-lewis/), first published in 1950. It is number 5 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). Four children step through a wardrobe and find a snowy land called Narnia.

## What is The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe about?

Four siblings are sent to a country house during the war. The youngest, Lucy, finds a wardrobe that opens into a snowy wood called Narnia, where it is always winter and never Christmas. The children discover that they have a part to play in the land’s fate.

It was published in 1950 and is the best-known of the seven Narnia books. The author, C.S. Lewis, was born in Belfast.

## Why children enjoy The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe

- A magical doorway into another world.
- Big ideas about courage and sacrifice, in a story that moves quickly.
- A series to move on to when children want more.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: where would your own wardrobe door lead?
- Draw a map of Narnia together.
- Read in winter, with a hot chocolate.

## About C.S. Lewis

C.S. Lewis, born Clive Staples Lewis on 29 November 1898 in Belfast, died on 22 November 1963. He was a scholar who taught at Oxford and Cambridge.
