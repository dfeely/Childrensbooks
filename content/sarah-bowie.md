**Sarah Bowie** is an author, illustrator and comics teacher. A Waterford author and illustrator who teaches comics-making. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Sarah Bowie

Sarah Bowie is an author and illustrator based in Waterford City. Her stories and comics have appeared in The Guardian and The Irish Times, and she teaches comics-making, visual storytelling and sketchnoting in schools, colleges and libraries.

She co-founded The Comics Lab with Debbie Jenkinson, and co-founded Ireland’s first Graphic Short Story Prize with The Irish Times. She also created the observational comic strip Seen and Heard in Dublin.

Her book **Nina Peanut: Major Drama Alert** is in our Top 20 children’s books list.
