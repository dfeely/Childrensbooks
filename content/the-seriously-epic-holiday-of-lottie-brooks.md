## What is The Seriously Epic Holiday of Lottie Brooks about?

Lottie Brooks tells her story as a humorous diary. This time she goes on a family skiing holiday after her former friend Amber invites her along. Lottie has never skied before, and the trip involves learning to ski with old and new friends, trying fondue for the first time and a storyline about a banana that Lottie believes is significant to her future.

It is part of the long-running Lottie Brooks series, where her friendships, crushes and embarrassments are told with plenty of doodles.

## Why children enjoy The Seriously Epic Holiday of Lottie Brooks

- A holiday setting gives a break from school and a fun change of scene.
- Lottie’s mishaps are relatable, from nerves on the slopes to friendship wobbles.
- Diary-style entries and drawings keep chapters short.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “What new thing would you be most nervous to try on holiday?”
- Make your own diary page about a holiday or day out.
- Compare Lottie’s first ski day with a time you tried something new.
