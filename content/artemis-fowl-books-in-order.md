**Artemis Fowl** is a fantasy adventure series by the Irish author Eoin Colfer. The first book was published in 2001 and follows a young criminal genius who sets out to steal fairy gold. Colfer, from Wexford, was Ireland’s Laureate na nÓg (children’s laureate) from 2014 to 2017. The books suit confident readers from about 9 or 10 and up.

## The Fowl Twins spin-offs

After the main series, Colfer wrote a spin-off trilogy about Artemis’s younger brothers: **The Fowl Twins** (2019), **The Fowl Twins: Deny All Charges** (2020) and **The Fowl Twins Get What They Deserve** (2021). They can be read after the main series.

## Which age is Artemis Fowl for?

Most children enjoy the series from about 9 to 12. The stories are fast and funny, with some peril and a lot of gadgets. Younger fans can listen to them read aloud.

### What order should I read the Artemis Fowl books in?
Read them in publication order, starting with Artemis Fowl and ending with The Last Guardian. The Fowl Twins books come after.

Eight in the main series, plus three Fowl Twins spin-off books.
