Classic Football Heroes: Best

The story of George Best, the Belfast boy who was told he was too small and became a Manchester United legend.

Classic Football Heroes: Best is a football biography title by Matt Oldfield. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 20. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Classic Football Heroes: Best about?

This is a children’s biography of George Best, the footballer from Belfast. It covers his early years, when he was overlooked for his small size, and his discovery by a Manchester United scout at 15. It follows his career as a midfielder and winger, including his time at Manchester United and his Ballon d’Or win.

It is part of the football series from Dino Books, in a Midfield Generals collection, and goes by the subtitle Wing Wizard in some listings.

Why children enjoy Classic Football Heroes: Best

  • Football fans who prefer true stories get an easy-to-read biography.
  • A Belfast hero gives the book extra interest for Irish readers.
  • The message that being small does not mean you cannot succeed is encouraging.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Ask: “Why did people overlook George Best at first?”
  • Compare the football of his day with today’s game.
  • Write a short biography of a player your child admires.
