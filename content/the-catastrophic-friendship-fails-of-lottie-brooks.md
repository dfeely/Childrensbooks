## What is The Catastrophic Friendship Fails of Lottie Brooks about?

Lottie starts the spring term at secondary school determined to stop trying to win over a mean girl and to leave an old embarrassing nickname behind. She lands a lead role in the spring musical, which gives her a chance to impress her crush, but her friendship with her two best friends begins to unravel despite her efforts to hold them together.

The main cast includes Lottie, Amber, Daniel, and Lottie’s best friends Molly and Jess. It is the second book in the series, after The Extremely Embarrassing Life of Lottie Brooks.

## Why children enjoy The Catastrophic Friendship Fails of Lottie Brooks

- A focus on friendship, a very relatable worry for tweens.
- Comic diary style with doodles.
- A good place for readers to start the series, since it builds on the first book.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “What would you do if two friends were arguing?”
- Talk about why friendship groups sometimes shift.
- Write a letter to a friend about something that matters.
