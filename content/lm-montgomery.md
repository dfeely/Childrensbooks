**L.M. Montgomery** is an author. The Canadian author of Anne of Green Gables. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About L.M. Montgomery

Lucy Maud Montgomery was born on 30 November 1874 in Clifton (now New London), Prince Edward Island, Canada, and died on 24 April 1942.

She published **Anne of Green Gables** in 1908 and followed it with sequels including Anne of Avonlea (1909). Her other books include Emily of New Moon (1923) and The Blue Castle (1926).
