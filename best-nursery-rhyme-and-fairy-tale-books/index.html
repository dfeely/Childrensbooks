Why nursery rhymes matter

Rhymes and songs help children hear the sounds in words, which supports later reading. Sing them, clap along and add actions.

Tips

  • Let your child fill in the last word of each line.
  • Add actions, such as twinkling fingers for the star.
  • Choose short fairy tales for toddlers and longer versions for older children.

For more, see our bedtime stories guide and Ladybird books.

What are the best nursery rhyme books?

Look for clear pictures, familiar rhymes and sturdy pages.

What age are fairy tales for?

Short retellings suit ages 2 to 6. Longer versions suit older children.

Where does Twinkle Twinkle Little Star come from?

The words come from a poem called The Star by Jane Taylor, published in 1806.

Are fairy tales too scary?

Some have scary moments. Read them first and choose gentler versions if needed.
