**Matilda** is a comic novel by [Roald Dahl](/roald-dahl/), illustrated by Quentin Blake, first published in 1988. It is number 6 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A bright girl with unkind parents finds a surprising power.

## What is Matilda about?

Matilda is a very clever little girl whose parents do not notice. She teaches herself to read and borrows books from the library. At school she meets a kind teacher, and a frightening headteacher, and finds she has a special power.

It was published in 1988 with illustrations by Quentin Blake.

## Why children enjoy Matilda

- A brilliant, funny heroine who loves books.
- Adults who are comically bad and children who win.
- Short, lively chapters that suit confident new readers.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: which book would Matilda borrow next?
- Visit your local library and pick your own pile.
- Read the funniest scene out loud with silly voices.

## About Roald Dahl

Roald Dahl was born on 13 September 1916 in Llandaff, Cardiff, Wales, to Norwegian parents, and died on 23 November 1990. He served as a fighter pilot in the Royal Air Force in the Second World War before becoming a writer.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
### What age is Matilda for?
It is usually recommended for ages 7 to 11, although many younger children enjoy it as a read-aloud.

### Who illustrated Matilda?
Quentin Blake, who illustrated most of Roald Dahl’s books.

### Where can I borrow Matilda?
