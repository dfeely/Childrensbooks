**Charlotte’s Web** is a classic novel by [E.B. White](/eb-white/), illustrated by Garth Williams, first published in 1952. It is number 4 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A young pig is saved by a clever spider in a story about friendship and loss.

## What is Charlotte’s Web about?

Wilbur, a young pig, is saved from the butcher by a girl named Fern. When he moves to a barn, he learns that he is still in danger, and a spider called Charlotte spins words into her web to help him.

It was published in 1952 with illustrations by Garth Williams. It is often one of the first novels children hear read aloud.

## Why children enjoy Charlotte’s Web

- A tender story about loyalty and friendship.
- It treats a sad moment honestly and kindly.
- The language is warm and rewards being read aloud.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: what makes Charlotte a good friend?
- Talk about the sad part gently if your child wants to.
- Read one chapter at a time to make it last.

## About E.B. White

E.B. White was born Elwyn Brooks White on 11 July 1899 in Mount Vernon, New York, and died on 1 October 1985. He wrote for The New Yorker and also wrote three children’s novels.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
### What age is Charlotte’s Web for?
It suits ages 6 to 10, and is a popular read-aloud for younger children.

### Is Charlotte’s Web sad?
It has a gentle, sad moment near the end. Many parents like to read it together so they can talk about it.
