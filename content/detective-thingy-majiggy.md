## What is Detective Thingy Majiggy about?

Ten-year-old Mia moves into a new foster home with Detective Lucy. She has a special gift: she can talk to everyday objects, and they talk back. With their help she investigates mysteries, including how a fire started in her new classroom.

The book has black-and-white illustrations throughout by Katie Saunders and is the start of a new series.

## Why children enjoy Detective Thingy Majiggy

- Talking objects make it playful and imaginative.
- A gentle mystery format with short chapters and illustrations suits newer readers.
- A foster-care setting gives a quietly warm view of finding a home.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “Which object in our house would be the best witness?”
- Invent a clue for your child to solve using household items.
- Talk about how Mia might feel in a new place.
