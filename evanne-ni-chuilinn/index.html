Evanne Ní Chuilinn

An Irish sports broadcaster who also writes for young readers.

Evanne Ní Chuilinn is a broadcaster and author. An Irish sports broadcaster who also writes for young readers. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books are in the current top 20 and where to start.

About Evanne Ní Chuilinn

Evanne Ní Chuilinn was born on 5 September 1981 in Kilkenny. She joined RTÉ in 2004 as a sub-editor in the sports department, later reporting for the programme OB Sport and as a sideline reporter for The Sunday Game, before moving to RTÉ News as a sports reporter and Six One sports presenter.

She presented the Irish-language Sunday Game Beo coverage of the 2023 All-Ireland hurling and football finals. She left RTÉ in February 2025 after being nominated to Seanad Éireann.

Her book Squad Girls appears in our Top 20 children’s books list.
