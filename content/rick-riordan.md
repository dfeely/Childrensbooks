**Rick Riordan** is an author. The American author of Percy Jackson and the Olympians. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books are in the current top 20 and where to start.

## About Rick Riordan

Rick Riordan was born in 1964 in San Antonio, Texas. He taught middle school in Texas and California for about 15 years before becoming a full-time writer.

His first Percy Jackson book, **The Lightning Thief**, was published in 2005. His other series include Heroes of Olympus, The Trials of Apollo, the Kane Chronicles and Magnus Chase and the Gods of Asgard, and he created the overall storyline for the 39 Clues series.
