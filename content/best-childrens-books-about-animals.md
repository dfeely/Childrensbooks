Children love animal stories. Animals can be funny, brave, silly or wise, and they let children explore feelings and friendships from a safe distance. This guide picks the best animal books on our site, from toddler favourites to comics and novels, and tells you what age each suits.

## For babies and toddlers (0 to 4)

:::books
### The Very Hungry Caterpillar
*By Eric Carle, 1969. Ages 0 to 4*

A caterpillar eats his way through the week and becomes a butterfly. It is a gentle book for counting and the days of the week. [Read our guide](/the-very-hungry-caterpillar/).

### Guess How Much I Love You
*By Sam McBratney, illustrated by Anita Jeram, 1994. Ages 0 to 4*

Two hares compete to say how much they love each other. It is a sweet, calm bedtime read. [Read our guide](/guess-how-much-i-love-you/).

### The Tiger Who Came to Tea
*By Judith Kerr, 1968. Ages 2 to 5*

A very hungry tiger comes to tea and eats everything. It is funny and short. [Read our guide](/the-tiger-who-came-to-tea/).
:::

## For preschool and early school (3 to 7)

:::books
### The Gruffalo
*By Julia Donaldson and Axel Scheffler, 1999. Ages 2 to 6*

A mouse tricks hungry animals with a made-up monster. The rhyme makes it great for joining in. [Read our guide](/the-gruffalo/).

### Gruffalo Granny
*By Julia Donaldson, illustrated by Axel Scheffler. Ages 3 to 7*

A new rhyming picture book from the creators of The Gruffalo, with a visit from a very special relative. [Read our guide](/gruffalo-granny/).

### The Tale of Peter Rabbit
*By Beatrix Potter, 1902. Ages 2 to 6*

A naughty rabbit goes into a garden he should not. It is a short classic with delicate pictures. [Read our guide](/the-tale-of-peter-rabbit/).

### Winnie-the-Pooh
*By A.A. Milne, illustrated by E.H. Shepard, 1926. Ages 4 to 8*

Gentle stories of a bear and his friends in the woods. They suit reading aloud. [Read our guide](/winnie-the-pooh/).

### Rabbit and Bear: The Bog of Frogs
*By Julian Gough, illustrated by Jim Field. Ages 5 and up*

Rabbit wonders what stars are made of and feels very small, until some new friends in a bog show him the bigger picture. By an Irish author. [Read our guide](/rabbit-and-bear-the-bog-of-frogs/).
:::

## For confident readers (6 to 10)

:::books
### Charlotte’s Web
*By E.B. White, illustrated by Garth Williams, 1952. Ages 6 to 10*

A pig is saved by a clever spider. It is a classic about friendship and loss. [Read our guide](/charlottes-web/).

### The Wind in the Willows
*By Kenneth Grahame, 1908. Ages 6 to 10*

Mole, Ratty, Badger and Toad share riverbank adventures. The language is old-fashioned, so it is best read aloud. [Read our guide](/the-wind-in-the-willows/).

### Bunny vs Monkey: Day of the Heroes
*By Jamie Smart. Ages 6 to 10 (suggested)*

The Woods need a hero in this comic adventure, good for readers who love graphic novels. [Read our guide](/bunny-vs-monkey-day-of-the-heroes/).

### Dog Man: 10th Birthday Edition
*By Dav Pilkey. Ages 6 to 9*

A graphic novel about a hero who is part dog and part police officer. It suits reluctant readers. [Read our guide](/dog-man-10th-birthday-edition/).

### Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama
*By Leona Forde. Ages 7 to 10 (suggested)*

Milly wants her own dog, and a dog-walking business grows into chaotic doggy daycare. [Read our guide](/milly-mccarthy-and-the-doggy-daycare-drama/).

### Disaster Dad: Animal Antics
*By Donncha O’Callaghan. Ages 7 and up*

Dad’s class trip to a wildlife park turns into chaos. Written by a former Ireland rugby international. [Read our guide](/disaster-dad-animal-antics/).
:::

## How to choose an animal book

- **Match the age.** Short rhyming books suit toddlers. Novels such as Charlotte’s Web are better when children can follow a longer story.
- **Look for humour.** Funny animals are often the ones children ask for again and again.
- **Read the pictures.** In picture books, the illustrations often tell their own story.
- **Link to real animals.** After reading, visit a farm, zoo or park and talk about the animals you saw. Our [Irish children’s authors guide](/irish-childrens-authors/) includes books set in Ireland.

:::faq
### What are the best animal books for toddlers?
The Very Hungry Caterpillar, Guess How Much I Love You and The Tiger Who Came to Tea are three good choices.

### What are the best animal books for 7 year olds?
Charlotte’s Web, The Wind in the Willows and comics such as Bunny vs Monkey and Dog Man are popular with this age.

### Are animal stories good for reading aloud?
Yes. Rhyming books such as The Gruffalo and classics such as Winnie-the-Pooh are especially good for reading aloud.

### Which animal books are Irish?
Rabbit and Bear, by Julian Gough, and Milly McCarthy, by Leona Forde, are from Irish authors.
