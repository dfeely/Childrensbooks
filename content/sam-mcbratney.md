**Sam McBratney** is an author. The Belfast-born author of Guess How Much I Love You. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Sam McBratney

Sam McBratney was born on 1 March 1943 in Belfast, Northern Ireland, and died on 18 September 2020 at the age of 77. He earned a history degree at Trinity College Dublin and taught in primary and secondary schools from 1970 to 1990, when he took early retirement to write full time.

**Guess How Much I Love You**, illustrated by Anita Jeram, was published in 1994 by Walker Books. His other books include Put a Saddle on the Pig (1991) and The Chieftain’s Daughter (1993).
