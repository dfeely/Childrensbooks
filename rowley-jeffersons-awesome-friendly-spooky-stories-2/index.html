Rowley Jefferson’s Awesome Friendly Spooky Stories 2

Rowley, Greg’s best friend from the Wimpy Kid books, tells a second set of spooky stories that are meant to make you laugh more than scream.

Rowley Jefferson’s Awesome Friendly Spooky Stories 2 is a spooky comic stories title by Jeff Kinney. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 6. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Rowley Jefferson’s Awesome Friendly Spooky Stories 2 about?

Rowley Jefferson, the cheerful friend from the Diary of a Wimpy Kid books, narrates a collection of spooky stories. There are monsters, creepy twists and goofy scares, all in a playful tone designed to raise giggles rather than nightmares.

This is the second collection, so readers who enjoyed the first can carry on, while newcomers can dip in because each tale stands alone.

Why children enjoy Rowley Jefferson’s Awesome Friendly Spooky Stories 2

  • A gentle way to try spooky stories without being too frightening.
  • Short tales fit into small amounts of reading time.
  • Rowley’s sunny voice is a contrast to Greg’s grumpiness in the main series.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Read it with the lights on for a first spooky story, then try it in the dark if everyone is brave.
  • Ask: “Which story was the silliest, and which was the creepiest?”
  • Invent your own friendly monster and draw it.

About Jeff Kinney

Jeff Kinney created Diary of a Wimpy Kid. The Awesome Friendly Kid books are Rowley’s own series, told in his upbeat voice.

Frequently asked questions about Rowley Jefferson’s Awesome Friendly Spooky Stories 2

Is Rowley Jefferson’s Awesome Friendly Spooky Stories 2 too scary?

The tone is playful and aimed at making readers laugh, so it is gentle for a spooky book. Very sensitive children may prefer to read it in daylight.

Do I need to read the first book?

No. It is a collection of separate stories, so you can start with this one.

What age is it suitable for?

We suggest around 7 to 11, especially for fans of the Wimpy Kid series.
