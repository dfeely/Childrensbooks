**Anne of Green Gables** is a classic novel by [L.M. Montgomery](/lm-montgomery/), first published in 1908. It is number 17 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). An imaginative orphan girl arrives at a farm on Prince Edward Island.

## What is Anne of Green Gables about?

Anne Shirley is sent to live with an elderly brother and sister who were expecting a boy. Chatty, imaginative and quick-tempered, she soon wins her way into their hearts and the community of Avonlea.

It was published in 1908 and is set on Prince Edward Island, Canada, where the author grew up. It has several sequels.

## Why children enjoy Anne of Green Gables

- A heroine with big dreams, big feelings and a vivid imagination.
- Humour and warmth alongside real emotion.
- A story that rewards readers who are ready for a longer read.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: what is the best name you could give your favourite place?
- Look up photos of Prince Edward Island together.
- Read the chapter where Anne makes a mistake and talk about what happens next.

## About L.M. Montgomery

Lucy Maud Montgomery was born on 30 November 1874 in Clifton (now New London), Prince Edward Island, Canada, and died on 24 April 1942.
