**E.B. White** is an author and essayist. The author of Charlotte’s Web and Stuart Little. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About E.B. White

E.B. White was born Elwyn Brooks White on 11 July 1899 in Mount Vernon, New York, and died on 1 October 1985. He wrote for The New Yorker and also wrote three children’s novels.

**Charlotte’s Web** was published in 1952 with pictures by Garth Williams. He also co-wrote the style guide The Elements of Style with William Strunk Jr.

## E.B. White’s books in our all-time classics list

- [Charlotte’s Web](/charlottes-web/) (1952): number 4 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A young pig is saved by a clever spider in a story about friendship and loss.

## Other well-known books and work

- Stuart Little (1945)
- Charlotte’s Web (1952)
- The Trumpet of the Swan (1970)
- The Elements of Style (with William Strunk Jr.)
