**Goodnight Moon** is a bedtime picture book by [Margaret Wise Brown](/margaret-wise-brown/), illustrated by Clement Hurd, first published in 1947. It is number 8 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A little rabbit says goodnight to everything in the great green room.

## What is Goodnight Moon about?

Before going to sleep, a little rabbit says goodnight to everything in his room, one by one, from the clocks and socks to the moon. The words are soft and slow, and the pictures get a little darker as the story goes on.

It was published in 1947 with pictures by Clement Hurd.

## Why children enjoy Goodnight Moon

- A hypnotic, lullaby-like rhythm that helps children wind down.
- Familiar household objects that toddlers can name and point to.
- A calm ending that signals sleep.

## Reading and talking tips

- Read it softly and slowly.
- Let your child say goodnight to things in their own room.
- Make it the last book of the night.

## About Margaret Wise Brown

Margaret Wise Brown was born on 23 May 1910 in Brooklyn, New York, and died on 13 November 1952. She wrote **Goodnight Moon** (1947), illustrated by Clement Hurd.

It is best for babies and toddlers, up to about age 3.

Margaret Wise Brown wrote it and Clement Hurd drew the pictures.
