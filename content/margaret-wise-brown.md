**Margaret Wise Brown** is an author. The author of the bedtime classic Goodnight Moon. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Margaret Wise Brown

Margaret Wise Brown was born on 23 May 1910 in Brooklyn, New York, and died on 13 November 1952. She wrote **Goodnight Moon** (1947), illustrated by Clement Hurd.

With Hurd she also made The Runaway Bunny (1942). She wrote many books for very young children, often in simple, musical language.

## Margaret Wise Brown’s books in our all-time classics list

- [Goodnight Moon](/goodnight-moon/) (1947): number 8 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A little rabbit says goodnight to everything in the great green room.
