**The Cat in the Hat** is a rhyming early reader by [Dr. Seuss](/dr-seuss/), first published in 1957. It is number 10 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A very rainy day turns upside down when a mischievous visitor arrives.

## What is The Cat in the Hat about?

Two children are alone at home on a rainy day when a tall, talkative cat turns up and starts causing mayhem. They must decide whether to stop the fun before their mother comes home.

It was published in 1957 and was written to be easier and more fun than the dull school readers of the time. Its rhymes and short words make it a long-standing favourite for early readers.

## Why children enjoy The Cat in the Hat

- Bouncing rhymes that make reading feel like a game.
- A funny, mischievous story that children enjoy again and again.
- Short, simple words that help beginner readers.

## Reading and talking tips

- Let your child read the rhyming word at the end of each line.
- Ask: would you let the cat in?
- Make a mess-free indoor game of your own.

## About Dr. Seuss

Dr. Seuss was the pen name of Theodor Seuss Geisel, born on 2 March 1904 in Springfield, Massachusetts, who died on 24 September 1991. He wrote and illustrated dozens of children’s books.

:::faq
### What age is The Cat in the Hat for?
It suits ages 3 to 7. Beginner readers often start to read it themselves around 5 or 6.

### Who wrote The Cat in the Hat?
Dr. Seuss, the pen name of Theodor Seuss Geisel.
