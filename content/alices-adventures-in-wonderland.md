**Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland** is a classic fantasy novel by [Lewis Carroll](/lewis-carroll/), illustrated by John Tenniel, first published in 1865. It is number 9 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A girl falls down a rabbit hole into a world of nonsense and puzzles.

## What is Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland about?

Alice follows a white rabbit down a hole and finds herself in a world where size, time and logic keep changing. She meets odd creatures, goes to a mad tea party and plays croquet with the Queen of Hearts.

It was published in 1865 under the pen name Lewis Carroll, with illustrations by John Tenniel. It began as a story the author told on a boat trip.

## Why children enjoy Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland

- Wordplay, riddles and nonsense that make children laugh.
- A heroine who keeps asking questions.
- Dozens of memorable scenes to talk about and act out.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: which part of Wonderland would you most like to visit?
- Hold a tea party with silly rules.
- Read a short chapter and talk about which words are made-up.

## About Lewis Carroll

Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, born on 27 January 1832 in Daresbury, Cheshire, who died on 14 January 1898. He was a mathematician who lectured at Christ Church, Oxford.

:::faq
### What age is Alice in Wonderland for?
It suits about age 7 and up. Younger children enjoy the pictures and an adapted version.

### Who was Lewis Carroll?
Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Dodgson, a mathematician and Oxford lecturer.
