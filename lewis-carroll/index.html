The pen name of the mathematician who wrote Alice in Wonderland.

About Lewis Carroll

Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, born on 27 January 1832 in Daresbury, Cheshire, who died on 14 January 1898. He was a mathematician who lectured at Christ Church, Oxford.

He first told the Alice story on a boat trip in 1862 and published Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland in 1865. It was followed by Through the Looking-Glass in 1871.
