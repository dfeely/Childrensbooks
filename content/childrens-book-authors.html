Frequently asked questions

Which authors are covered?

All 33 authors behind our Top 20 children’s books and our best children’s books of all time, from Jeff Kinney and Julia Donaldson to Beatrix Potter, Roald Dahl and Irish writers such as Cecelia Ahern and Leona Forde.

Are the author pictures photographs?

No. Every portrait is our own illustration of a generic writer at work. They are not likenesses of the real authors.

Where do the biographies come from?

From publisher biographies, reference works, interviews and authors’ own websites. Dates and details can change, so check a publisher’s site for the latest.
