**Percy Jackson and the Olympians** is a series of five fantasy novels by [Rick Riordan](/rick-riordan/), starting with *The Lightning Thief*, published in 2005. They follow a boy who learns that the Greek gods are real and that he is the son of one of them. They suit readers aged about 9 to 13.

## What to read after the five books

Riordan’s later series share the same world: **The Heroes of Olympus**, **The Trials of Apollo**, and others. A newer story in the Percy Jackson world, [Camp Half-Blood: The Wild Zone](/camp-half-blood-the-wild-zone/), is in our current Top 20 children’s books.

## Which age is Percy Jackson for?

About 9 to 13. The stories are fast-moving and funny, with action and some scary moments. They make a good read-aloud for younger children if you are happy to skip the scariest bits.

## If you like Percy Jackson

Try [Artemis Fowl](/artemis-fowl-books-in-order/), [The Hobbit](/the-hobbit/) and [Harry Potter](/harry-potter-and-the-philosophers-stone/).
