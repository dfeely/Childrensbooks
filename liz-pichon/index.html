Liz Pichon

The author and illustrator of the Tom Gates series.

Liz Pichon is an author and illustrator. The author and illustrator of the Tom Gates series. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

About Liz Pichon

Liz Pichon is a British author and illustrator who studied graphic design at Camberwell School of Art. In the late 1980s she worked as an art director at Jive Records, before becoming a children’s author and illustrator.

Her Tom Gates series began in 2011 and now runs to more than 20 main books, with about 16.5 million copies sold according to her publisher’s figures.

Tom Gates won the Roald Dahl Funny Prize in 2011 and Liz also won the Blue Peter Book Award for Best Story.
