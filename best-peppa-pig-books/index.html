Best Peppa Pig books for toddlers

Board books and bedtime stories for fans of the muddy-puddle-loving pig.

Peppa Pig is a British animated series, created by Neville Astley and Mark Baker, which began in 2004. The books follow Peppa, her little brother George and their family and friends through everyday adventures. They suit children aged about 1 to 5.

Which Peppa book should I choose?

  • Board books are best for babies and toddlers.
  • Picture books suit bedtime and story time.
  • Pick a story that matches something your child is doing, such as visiting the library or washing day.
