**Leona Forde** is an author and teacher. A Cork teacher who writes the Milly McCarthy books. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Leona Forde

Leona Forde was born in 1979 in Ballyphehane, Cork, and lives in Bandon, West Cork. She is a teacher and writer who, at the time of a newspaper interview, had been teaching for about 20 years.

She began writing during lockdown while taking writing courses. Her daughter had noticed that most of her books were set in America or Britain, so Leona began making up stories set closer to home. Her debut, **Milly McCarthy Is a Complete Catastrophe**, was illustrated by Karen Harte and sends Milly’s class on a tour of Fota Wildlife Park.

The second book, Milly McCarthy and the Irish Dancing Disaster (2023), was shortlisted for the An Post Specsavers Children’s Book of the Year awards.
