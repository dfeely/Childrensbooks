**Dav Pilkey** is an author and illustrator. The creator of Dog Man and Captain Underpants. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Dav Pilkey

Dav Pilkey is the author and illustrator of the **Dog Man** and **Captain Underpants** series. As a child he was diagnosed with ADHD and dyslexia, and teachers often had him sit in the hallway, where he drew and made up stories. His publisher’s biography says the first comic adventures of Dog Man and Captain Underpants were created there.

His other work includes Ricky Ricotta’s Mighty Robot, Cat Kid Comic Club, Dumb Bunnies, Super Diaper Baby, Dogzilla and Kat Kong. The Paperboy was a Caldecott Honor book. His books have sold millions of copies and been translated into many languages.
