The Tiger Who Came to Tea

A surprise visitor knocks at the door at teatime and eats everything in the house.

The Tiger Who Came to Tea is a picture book by Judith Kerr, first published in 1968. It is number 18 in our list of the best children’s books of all time. A surprise visitor knocks at the door at teatime and eats everything in the house.

What is The Tiger Who Came to Tea about?

A girl called Sophie and her mother are about to have tea when there is a ring at the door. Their visitor is a large, polite tiger, who is hungry. He eats all the food and drinks everything in the house.

It was published in 1968. Judith Kerr both wrote and illustrated it.

Why children enjoy The Tiger Who Came to Tea

  • A funny, surprising story told with simple words.
  • Everyday settings that young children recognise.
  • A story about being kind to an unexpected guest.

Reading and talking tips

  • Ask: what would you do if someone knocked at your door?
  • Name the food the visitor eats.
  • Have a toy tea party.

About Judith Kerr

Judith Kerr was born on 14 June 1923 in Berlin and died on 22 May 2019 in London. As a child she fled Nazi Germany with her family and later lived in Britain.
