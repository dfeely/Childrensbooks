Guides for raising readers

Short, practical guides on choosing books, reading aloud and keeping children reading, written for busy families.

Raising a reader does not need special training or lots of time. It needs a few good habits and books children want to pick up. These guides cover the questions parents and carers ask most.

The five habits of reading families

  1. They read aloud, every day. Even ten minutes counts, and it works for children of all ages.
  2. They keep books within reach. A basket in the living room beats a shelf in the attic.
  3. They let children choose. Choice builds ownership and motivation.
  4. They go to the library. It is free, fun and full of surprises.
  5. They are seen reading. Children copy what adults do, so let them see you with a book.
