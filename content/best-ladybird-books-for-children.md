## Fairy tales and rhymes (ages 2 to 5)

The Ladybird Tales retell traditional stories in short, clear text. Pair them with nursery rhyme books for first story times. See our guide to [nursery rhyme and fairy tale books](/best-nursery-rhyme-and-fairy-tale-books/).

## Early reading (ages 4 to 7)

The Key Words books and flashcards are designed to help children learn common words step by step. Use them alongside picture books and shared reading. See [books for early readers](/books-for-early-readers/).

## Tips

- Read the story together first, then let your child read the repeated words.
- Keep sessions short and fun.
- Check the level and age before you order.
