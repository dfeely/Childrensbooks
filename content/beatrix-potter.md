**Beatrix Potter** is an author and illustrator. The writer and illustrator of The Tale of Peter Rabbit. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Beatrix Potter

Beatrix Potter was born on 28 July 1866 in London and died on 22 December 1943. She began Peter Rabbit in a picture letter in 1893, had it printed privately in 1901 and saw it published by Frederick Warne in 1902.

She later farmed in the Lake District and left a great deal of land to the National Trust.

## Beatrix Potter’s books in our all-time classics list
