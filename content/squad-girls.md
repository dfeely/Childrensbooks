**Squad Girls** is a sport and friendship fiction title by Evanne Ní Chuilinn. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 8. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

## What is Squad Girls about?

Squad Girls follows four friends, Tara, Ríona, Sam and Penny, as they juggle school, friendships and their passions for sport and dance. Between them they take on sprinting, swimming, Irish dancing and gymnastics.

It is the first in a series, and Children’s Books Ireland has recommended it. Readers who enjoy contemporary school stories with a sporting backbone will find plenty here.

## Why children enjoy Squad Girls

- Different sports and dance styles mean most readers will find someone to relate to.
- An Irish setting and Irish dancing give it local flavour.
- A friendship focus with real-feeling problems suits readers moving into tween fiction.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Ask: “Which of the four friends is most like you?”
- Talk about how training and teamwork feel in your own activities.
- If your child does a sport or dance, compare it with the book’s descriptions.
