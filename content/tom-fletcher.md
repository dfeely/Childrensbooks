**Tom Fletcher** is an author and musician. A founding member of McFly who writes bestselling children’s books. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Tom Fletcher

Tom Fletcher is a founding member of the Brit Award-winning band McFly and one of the UK’s most popular children’s authors. His debut novel, **The Christmasaurus** (2016), was the biggest children’s debut of its year and was shortlisted for a British Book Award.

He has since written The Christmasaurus and the Naughty List, The Creakers and **The Danger Gang**, and writes the Who’s in Your Book? picture books. He co-writes the Dinosaur That Pooped series with Dougie Poynter and the Eve of Man series with his wife, Giovanna Fletcher, and wrote the songs for Paddington: The Musical.

His publisher says his books have been translated into 42 languages. **The Danger Game** is illustrated by Shane Devries, an illustrator from Adelaide, Australia.
