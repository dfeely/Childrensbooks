**J.R.R. Tolkien** is an author and professor. The author of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About J.R.R. Tolkien

J.R.R. Tolkien was born John Ronald Reuel Tolkien on 3 January 1892 in Bloemfontein, South Africa, and died on 2 September 1973. He was a professor at Oxford.

**The Hobbit** was published in 1937, and he went on to write The Lord of the Rings (1954 to 1955). The Silmarillion was published after his death.

## J.R.R. Tolkien’s books in our all-time classics list

- [The Hobbit](/the-hobbit/) (1937): number 13 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). A small, comfort-loving hobbit is swept into a quest to win back a treasure from a dragon.
