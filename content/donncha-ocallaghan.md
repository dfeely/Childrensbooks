**Donncha O’Callaghan** is a former rugby international and author. A former Munster and Ireland rugby player who writes the Disaster Dad books. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books are in the current top 20 and where to start.

## About Donncha O’Callaghan

Donncha O’Callaghan is a former Munster and Ireland rugby international who is now a radio presenter and television fitness coach.

His **Disaster Dad** children’s series is based on real mishaps from his own life. The first book, Disaster Dad: Chicken Chaos, came out in 2024, and the second, Fun Day Fiasco, was written with Karen Owen and illustrated by Jenny Taylor. He has said he wants children to see that embarrassing moments happen and that how you handle them matters.
