## What is Bunny vs Monkey: Day of the Heroes about?

The Woods are in trouble. A greased pig needs rescuing, some of the residents are stuck in jelly, and a zombie invasion is spreading. Bunny and friends have to save the day, though their rivalry has a habit of getting in the way.

Jamie Smart’s comics are fast, silly and packed with jokes, so every page has something to point at. It is a comic book rather than a prose novel, with panels, speech bubbles and plenty of slapstick.

## Why children enjoy Bunny vs Monkey: Day of the Heroes

- Comic panels keep pages moving, which suits readers who find big blocks of text a barrier.
- The humour is daft and quick, with jokes on the sides of the pictures for sharp-eyed readers.
- Because the cast is large, there is always a favourite character to follow.

## Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

- Take turns reading the speech bubbles, one person per character.
- Ask: “Which character would you want on your team?”
- Look for the small background jokes together.

## About Jamie Smart

Jamie Smart is a British cartoonist whose Bunny vs Monkey strips first appeared in The Phoenix, the weekly children’s comic, before being collected into books.

## Frequently asked questions about Bunny vs Monkey: Day of the Heroes

:::faq
### What age is Bunny vs Monkey for?
We suggest about 6 to 10. The comic format and short scenes make it approachable for newer readers.

### Is Bunny vs Monkey scary because of the zombies?
The zombie invasion is played for laughs and drawn in a cartoon style, so it is more silly than scary. Sensitive children may still want a grown-up nearby for the first read.

### Do I need the earlier Bunny vs Monkey books?
The stories are self-contained comic adventures, so you can start with this one, though the earlier collections give more background on the characters.
:::
