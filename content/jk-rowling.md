**J.K. Rowling** is an author. The British author of the Harry Potter series. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About J.K. Rowling

J.K. Rowling was born in 1965 in Yate, Gloucestershire, England. She wrote **Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone**, first published by Bloomsbury in 1997, as the first of seven main books about the boy wizard.

She has also written Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them and The Tales of Beedle the Bard, the children’s story The Ickabog, and crime novels for adults under the pen name Robert Galbraith.

## J.K. Rowling’s books in our all-time classics list

- [Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone](/harry-potter-and-the-philosophers-stone/) (1997): number 1 in our [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). The first book about a boy who learns he is a wizard and starts at a school of magic.

## Other well-known books and work

- Harry Potter (seven main books, 1997 to 2007)
- Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them
- The Ickabog
- The Cormoran Strike crime novels, as Robert Galbraith

## Where to start

Start with Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone.
