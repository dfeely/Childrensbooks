**Neill Cameron** is a cartoonist and comic writer. The cartoonist behind Donut Squad and Mega Robo Bros. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About Neill Cameron

Neill Cameron is a British cartoonist, born in 1977. His work has appeared regularly in the weekly comic The Phoenix since 2011, where **Mega Robo Bros** and **Donut Squad** both began before being collected as books.

He is also the author of How to Make Awesome Comics (2014, David Fickling Books), a how-to guide that grew out of his Phoenix strip and has a new edition in 2026. Children who like drawing comics often find it a good next step.

## Neill Cameron’s books in the top 20

- [Donut Squad: Destiny of the Donuts!](/donut-squad-destiny-of-the-donuts/): number 7 in the list we checked on 7 November 2026. A war with the Bagels spans time itself as the Donut Squad travels back to change history.

## Other well-known books and work

- Mega Robo Bros
- Bulldog Empire
- Tamsin (serialised in The Phoenix, 2017 to 2018)
- How to Make Awesome Comics (2014)

## Awards and recognition

- Winner of the Excelsior Award Jr in 2017 for Mega Robo Bros
- Donut Squad: Take Over the World! was shortlisted for a Waterstones Book of the Year award in 2025, according to his Wikipedia entry

## Where to start

Start with Donut Squad for silly adventure, or Mega Robo Bros for a story about two brothers and a robot.
