Disaster Dad: Animal Antics

Dad’s class trip to a wildlife park turns into chaos when he tries to get a newborn giraffe named after him.

Disaster Dad: Animal Antics is a comic family fiction title by Donncha O’Callaghan. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 5. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Disaster Dad: Animal Antics about?

Finn and Emma’s dad goes along on the class trip to a wildlife park. His plan is to photograph a newly born giraffe and have it named after him. Finn and Emma have to keep him out of trouble long enough for everyone to meet the giraffe.

It follows earlier Disaster Dad books such as Chicken Chaos and is built around the idea that a well-meaning grown-up can embarrass a child more than anyone else can.

Why children enjoy Disaster Dad: Animal Antics

  • Children enjoy watching an adult make the mistakes for once.
  • A school trip setting is familiar, and the animal theme adds appeal.
  • A famous Irish name on the cover gives it extra pull at home.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Ask: “What is the most embarrassing thing a parent has ever done?” and be ready for answers.
  • Look up giraffes together after reading and see which facts surprise you.
  • Take turns reading Dad’s lines in your silliest voice.

About Donncha O’Callaghan

Donncha O’Callaghan is a former Ireland and Munster rugby player. The Disaster Dad series draws on the idea of a dad whose enthusiasm keeps getting him into trouble.

Frequently asked questions about Disaster Dad: Animal Antics

What age is Disaster Dad: Animal Antics for?

The publisher suggests 7 and up.

Who is Donncha O’Callaghan?

He is a former Irish rugby international who played for Munster and Ireland. The Disaster Dad books are his series for young readers.

Should I read the other Disaster Dad books first?

You can start here. Earlier books, such as Chicken Chaos, give more background on the family but are not required.
