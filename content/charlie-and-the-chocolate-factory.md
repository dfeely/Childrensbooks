**Charlie and the Chocolate Factory** is a comic fantasy novel by [Roald Dahl](/roald-dahl/), illustrated by Quentin Blake, first published in 1964. It is number 15 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). Five children win golden tickets to see inside Willy Wonka’s famous chocolate factory.

## What is Charlie and the Chocolate Factory about?

Charlie Bucket is a poor boy who lives with his family near a mysterious chocolate factory. When he wins one of five golden tickets, he joins a tour with four other children and the eccentric Willy Wonka.

It was first published in 1964. Later editions were illustrated by Quentin Blake.

## Why children enjoy Charlie and the Chocolate Factory

- A world of sweets and inventions that children love.
- Fast, funny chapters with a clear moral.
- A natural book to share before or after the films.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: what would you invent in a chocolate factory?
- Compare the book with the films, if you have seen them.
- Make your own golden ticket.

## About Roald Dahl

Roald Dahl was born on 13 September 1916 in Llandaff, Cardiff, Wales, to Norwegian parents, and died on 23 November 1990. He served as a fighter pilot in the Royal Air Force in the Second World War before becoming a writer.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
### What age is Charlie and the Chocolate Factory for?
It suits ages 6 to 10 and is a good read-aloud for younger children.

### Who illustrated it?
The first editions had other illustrators, and Quentin Blake illustrated later editions.
