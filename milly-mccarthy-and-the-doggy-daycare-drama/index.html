Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama

Milly wants her own dog, and a dog-walking business grows into chaotic doggy daycare.

Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama is a comic children’s fiction title by Leona Forde. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 9. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama about?

Milly wants her own dog and sets out to prove she is responsible by starting a dog-walking business. The job grows into a doggy daycare when she ends up looking after a spotted Dalmatian, a clever poodle and a lost labradoodle.

It is part of the Milly McCarthy series, which also includes titles such as Milly McCarthy Is a Complete Catastrophe, Milly McCarthy and the Sports Day Shambles and Milly McCarthy and the Irish Dancing Disaster.

Why children enjoy Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama

  • Dog lovers will be hooked by the animals alone.
  • Milly’s well-meant schemes keep going wrong in ways children find funny.
  • The Irish setting and humour feel familiar to readers here.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Ask: “What would you need to look after a dog properly?”
  • Talk about how Milly proves she is responsible.
  • Draw a doggy daycare of your own with a rulebook.

Frequently asked questions about Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama

What age is Milly McCarthy and the Doggy Daycare Drama for?

The publisher lists it as children’s fiction without a set age. We suggest around 7 to 10.

Do I need to read the other Milly McCarthy books first?

No. This is part of a series, but the story works on its own.

How long is the book?

The paperback is 176 pages, which makes it a manageable length for newer chapter-book readers.
