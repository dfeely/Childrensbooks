Dog Man: 10th Birthday Edition

A special foil-covered paperback of the first Dog Man book, celebrating ten years of the part-dog, part-police-officer hero.

Dog Man: 10th Birthday Edition is a graphic novel title by Dav Pilkey. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 17. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Dog Man: 10th Birthday Edition about?

This is a paperback 10th birthday edition of the first Dog Man book, marked by a shiny foil cover. In the story, George and Harold, the heroes of the Captain Underpants books, create a comic about a hero who is part dog and part man. After a dog and a police officer are hurt by the villainous Petey the Cat, a surgery saves them both, and the result is Dog Man.

Dog Man detects deception, chases criminals and goes after squirrels. The story asks whether he can resist his wild instincts to do his duty.

Why children enjoy Dog Man: 10th Birthday Edition

  • Full-colour comic panels and short, funny scenes suit new readers.
  • Themes include empathy, kindness, persistence and staying true to yourself.
  • It is a long-running series, so one book can lead to many more.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Ask: “Why do you think Dog Man wants to do the right thing even when it is hard?”
  • Make your own comic strip with a hero who is part animal.
  • Compare the comics George and Harold make with the way the book itself is drawn.

About Dav Pilkey

Dav Pilkey is the American creator of Captain Underpants and Dog Man. Scholastic says the Dog Man series has sold more than 70 million copies in 50 languages.

Frequently asked questions about Dog Man: 10th Birthday Edition

What age is Dog Man for?

Dog Man books are popular with children from around 6 to 9, and many older reluctant readers enjoy them too.

Is the 10th birthday edition a new story?

No. It is the first Dog Man book in a special foil-covered paperback edition.

Is Dog Man good for reluctant readers?

Yes. The comic panels, humour and short chapters make it a favourite for children who do not usually reach for books.
