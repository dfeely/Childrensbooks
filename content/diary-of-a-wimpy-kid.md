**Diary of a Wimpy Kid** is a illustrated diary novel by [Jeff Kinney](/jeff-kinney/), first published in 2007. It is number 20 in our list of the [best children’s books of all time](/best-childrens-books-of-all-time/). An illustrated journal of a boy’s first year at middle school.

## What is Diary of a Wimpy Kid about?

Greg Heffley starts middle school and records his troubles, schemes and mistakes in a journal. He wants to be popular, but his plans have a way of going wrong.

The first printed book was published in 2007 after an online version in 2004. It uses simple cartoons alongside handwritten-style text, which many reluctant readers find welcoming.

## Why children enjoy Diary of a Wimpy Kid

- Short, funny entries that are easy to dip into.
- Cartoon drawings on almost every page.
- A good hook for children who say they do not like reading.

## Reading and talking tips

- Ask: what would be in your own diary?
- Draw a diary page of your own with cartoons.
- Read a few entries aloud and compare notes.

## About Jeff Kinney

Jeff Kinney is an American author and cartoonist, born in 1971 in Fort Washington, Maryland. He is best known for **Diary of a Wimpy Kid**, the illustrated journal of schoolboy Greg Heffley.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
### What age is Diary of a Wimpy Kid for?
It suits about ages 8 to 12.

### Is it good for reluctant readers?
Yes. The short entries and cartoons make it popular with children who find long text hard.
