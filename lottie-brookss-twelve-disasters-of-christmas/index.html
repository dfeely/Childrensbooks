Lottie Brooks’s Twelve Disasters of Christmas

Twelve festive stories in which Christmas plans go wrong, with Lottie telling her own most disastrous one.

Lottie Brooks’s Twelve Disasters of Christmas is a festive short stories title by Katie Kirby. In the children’s bestseller list we checked on 7 November 2026, it was number 4. This guide explains what the book is about, who it suits, how to talk about it and what to read next.

What is Lottie Brooks’s Twelve Disasters of Christmas about?

This festive collection holds twelve Christmas stories, most of them centred on characters other than Lottie. The tales include a first Christmas, a prank that backfires, a child who becomes the star of a school show and a girl who helps her football team. Lottie also narrates her own most disastrous Christmas.

It is a standalone treat for fans of the Lottie Brooks diaries, and because each story is short, it is a good pick for a busy December when reading time comes in small pieces.

Why children enjoy Lottie Brooks’s Twelve Disasters of Christmas

  • Short stories suit a December routine, so you can finish one in a single sitting.
  • Familiar characters such as Molly, Jess, Bella, Toby, Poppy and Burger Tom pop up, as does Lottie’s cousin Fwankie.
  • The humour is warm and embarrassing in a way tweens recognise.

Reading and talking tips

Use these prompts to make reading time a conversation:

  • Read one story a night as a countdown, like a mini advent calendar.
  • Ask: “Which disaster would be the worst to have at Christmas?”
  • Let your child pick the order, since the stories stand alone.

About Katie Kirby

Katie Kirby writes the Lottie Brooks series, comic diaries about the ups and downs of life as a tween, illustrated with drawings throughout.

Frequently asked questions about Lottie Brooks’s Twelve Disasters of Christmas

What age is Lottie Brooks’s Twelve Disasters of Christmas for?

The publisher suggests ages 9 to 12.

Is this one of the numbered Lottie Brooks diaries?

No. It is a festive collection of twelve stories, so you can read it without following the main diaries in order.
