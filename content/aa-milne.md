**A.A. Milne** is an author and playwright. The author of Winnie-the-Pooh. This page is a short guide for parents, teachers and young readers: who they are, which of their books we recommend and where to start.

## About A.A. Milne

A.A. Milne was born Alan Alexander Milne on 18 January 1882 in London and died on 31 January 1956. He wrote for the magazine Punch and was also a playwright.

**Winnie-the-Pooh** (1926) was inspired by his son Christopher Robin’s toys. It was illustrated by E.H. Shepard and followed by The House at Pooh Corner in 1928, with the poetry collections When We Were Very Young (1924) and Now We Are Six (1927).
