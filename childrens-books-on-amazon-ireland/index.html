Tips before you buy

  • Check the age. Board books suit babies and toddlers, picture books suit about 2 to 7, and novels suit confident readers.
  • Look at the format. Paperbacks are cheaper, hardbacks last longer and board books survive chewing.
  • Borrow first. If you are unsure, try it from the library. See our guide to children’s books and your local library.
  • Want more ideas? See our gift guide, the best children’s books of all time and the Top 20 children’s books.
